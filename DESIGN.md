## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the playful refinement and sensory indulgence of an artisanal Parisian pâtisserie blended with the warmth of a boutique Middle Eastern confectionery. Designed primarily for mobile-first dessert enthusiasts, gifting patrons, and luxury sweet lovers, the interface avoids sterile tech minimalism in favor of an inviting, sensory-rich aesthetic termed **Boutique Confectionery Elegance**.

The emotional tone balances nostalgic warmth with polished gastronomic luxury. Every interaction conveys joy, care, and delicate craftsmanship:
- **Atmosphere:** Warm marshmallow surfaces, creamy gelato pastel accents, and tactile curves replace sharp clinical boxes.
- **Directionality:** Strict Right-to-Left (RTL) orientation across all surfaces, ensuring balanced typographic flow, natural thumb geometry, and culturally resonant visual rhythm.
- **Micro-tactility:** Pill-shaped tactile interactive elements, floating card docks, soft transitions, and warm berry-tinted depth give elements a soft, confection-like touch.

## Colors

The color palette intentionally avoids synthetic cold grays, sterile whites, and neutral blacks. Depth and tone are grounded in edible gourmet textures: roasted cacao, raspberry wine, wild strawberry, and sweet gelato cream pastels.

### Foundations & Canvas
- **Warm Marshmallow Cream (`#FFFAF8`):** The default root canvas, giving the entire viewport a rich, soft confectionery foundation without harsh sterile glare.
- **Pure Pâtisserie White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-tier elevated surfaces, popovers, image cradles, and floating dock bars.
- **Soft Marshmallow Tint (`#FFF5F8`):** Subtle internal input fills, soft chip states, and secondary wells.
- **Delicate Wafer Line (`#F4E1E6`):** Low-contrast structural border separating sections and outlining product cards.
- **Deep Wafer Line (`#F6DDE5`):** Structural borders for floating elements and active container perimeters.

### Core Accents
- **Deep Royal Berry (`#A02857`):** The primary brand anchor. Powers official brand typography, prominent pricing numerals, authoritative headers, and selected toggle outlines.
- **Vibrant Strawberry (`#D72D58`):** The primary interactive CTA color. Powers primary action pills, floating badges, active navigation states, and checkout triggers.
- **Confectionery Rose (`#B85C6E`):** Tertiary tonal color for decorative heart flourishes, category subtitles, and icon glyph accents.
- **Dark Cacao Ink (`#3B2A2F`):** The default typography ink. Delivers exceptional readability while preserving warm edible warmth over pure `#000000`.
- **Warm Truffle Taupe (`#8D7A80`):** Secondary text color for strikethrough prices, item counts, metadata, and placeholder copy.

### Gelato Pastels Matrix
Used cyclically across product vitrines, showcase badges, and seasonal categories:
- **Strawberry Milk:** `#F9DCD9`
- **Pistachio Mint:** `#DFF0DC`
- **Marshmallow Rose:** `#FBD3DD`
- **Vanilla Wafer:** `#FBECC8`
- **Peach Sorbet:** `#F6E0D2`
- **Cotton Candy Blue:** `#E2EEFB`

### Semantic Highlights
- **Amber Star (`#E9A93A`):** Customer rating stars and confectionery awards.
- **Fresh Mint Soft (`#E3F6F1`):** In-stock inventory capsule fill with **Forest Mint (`#2F8A6D`)** text.
- **WhatsApp Concierge (`#25D366`):** Direct support channel trigger.

## Typography

The typography architecture uses a dual-font Arabic hierarchy that pairs the calligraphic, artisanal elegance of **El Messiri** with the crisp, modern geometric precision of **IBM Plex Sans Arabic**.

### Structural Principles
- **Title & Display (`El Messiri`):** Evokes traditional pastry calligraphy, luxury boutique menus, and celebratory gifting. Used for main landing heroes, category vitrine headers, product names on cards, and dialog titles.
- **Body & Numerical Interface (`IBM Plex Sans Arabic`):** High x-height and open optical counters guarantee effortless scanning on high-density mobile screens. Used for ingredients, product specifications, action buttons, category filter tabs, badges, and numbers.
- **Line Height Guidance:** Arabic diacritical marks require breathing room. Maintain line heights between 1.3 and 1.4 for headers, and 1.65 to 1.7 for descriptive body text to prevent visual collisions.
- **Price Format:** Prices are expressed in high-contrast Western/Arabic digits using Extrabold 800 weight with standard Arabic currency symbols (e.g., `ر.س`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 4px baseline rhythm optimized for mobile dessert browsing and desktop gourmet showcase vitrines.

### Grid System & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** Fixed-width centered container (`max-width: 1200px`) using 12 fluid grid columns with `gutter` (20px / 1.25rem) and generous canvas margin (24px / 1.5rem). Product showcase lists run as `repeat(auto-fill, minmax(240px, 1fr))`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** Fluid layout with 8 columns, 16px gutters, and 20px outer margin. Product cards display in 3 columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Strict 2-column layout (`repeat(2, 1fr)`) with `gutter-mobile` (12px / 0.75rem) and `margin-mobile` (16px / 1rem). Provides high visual density while keeping tap boundaries accessible.

### Vertical Spacing & Section Breaks
- Sections maintain airy gaps of 48px to 72px on desktop and 32px to 40px on mobile.
- Section enclosures make generous use of organic wavy transition dividers to bridge the marshmallow cream canvas with promotional sections.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through **ambient warm berry shadows** rather than neutral black or cold gray shadows. All shadows carry a hint of raspberry and rose, producing a warm glow that integrates with the marshmallow cream canvas.

### Depth Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat):** Inline controls, search fields in resting state, table rows, and neutral tab bars. Outlined cleanly with Delicate Wafer Line (`#F4E1E6`).
- **Level 1 (Ambient Resting):** Subtle lift for resting product cards, icon action circles, and announcement capsules.
  - Shadow: `0 4px 14px rgba(184, 92, 110, 0.08)`
- **Level 2 (Active Hover & Interactive Focus):** Triggered when hovering over product cards or engaging floating category chips. Product cards lift vertically by `-8px` while internal images scale to `1.06`.
  - Shadow: `0 14px 34px rgba(184, 92, 110, 0.14)`
- **Level 3 (Floating Docks, Drawers & Modals):** Reserved for the mobile bottom dock bar, flyout shopping cart drawer, and gift customization modals.
  - Shadow: `0 26px 60px rgba(124, 29, 67, 0.18)`
- **Action Glow (Primary CTA Elevation):** High-energy tactile vibrancy applied directly to Strawberry buttons and selected badges.
  - Shadow: `0 12px 26px rgba(215, 45, 88, 0.30)`

## Shapes

The design system uses a pill-dominant geometry (`roundedness: 3`) to echo the physical forms of dragees, macarons, and bonbons.

### Geometric Scale
- **Pill Capsules (`999px`):** Used universally across primary action buttons, filter tags, search bars, price badges, and quantity selectors.
- **Rounded Product Cards (`22px`):** Signature card corner radius that softens product grids and prevents sharp edges.
- **Major Section Containers (`32px`):** Large decorative wrapper pods, hero showcase frames, and modal sheets.
- **Nested Media Cradles (`18px`):** Product image wells nested within the 22px cards, ensuring harmonious concentric curvature.
- **Circular Touchpoints (`50%`):** Quick-action circular icons (favorites, cart quick-add, back buttons).

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Touchpoints
- **Primary CTA Button:** Pill capsule (`min-height: 52px`, `padding: 0 28px`). Solid Vibrant Strawberry (`#D72D58`) fill with crisp white text in Extrabold weight (`label-lg`). Casts the signature glowing strawberry shadow. On hover/active, transitions to Deep Berry (`#7C1D43`) with `transform: translateY(-2px)`.
- **Secondary Outlined Button:** Pill capsule with Pure White fill, 1.5px Candy Blush border (`#FED7E7`), and Vibrant Strawberry text. Hover state gently fills with Strawberry Milk (`#F9DCD9`).
- **Quick-Add Card Button:** Compact pill (`height: 40px`, `padding: 0 20px`). Pure white surface, Deep Royal Berry text, and Level 1 shadow. On card hover or tap, inverts into solid Vibrant Strawberry with white text.
- **Circular Action Icon:** 46px desktop / 40px mobile circular button with Delicate Wafer border (`#F4E1E6`). Houses heart (wishlist), share, or close glyphs.

### Chips & Filter Tabs
- **Filter Segmented Tabs:** Pill capsule container wrapped in Pure White with a 1px wafer border. Active segment receives a vibrant berry gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #EF4F78 0%, #D72D58 45%, #A02857 100%)`) with white text and Action Glow shadow.
- **Pill Category Chips:** Horizontal scrollable rail. Inactive chips use Soft Marshmallow Tint (`#FFF5F8`) with Dark Cacao text; active chips use solid Vibrant Strawberry with white text.

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** `22px` border radius, sequentially rotating through the 6 Gelato Pastels (`#F9DCD9`, `#DFF0DC`, `#FBD3DD`, `#FBECC8`, `#F6E0D2`, `#E2EEFB`). Internal padding is `14px 14px 16px`.
- **Image Cradle:** Semi-translucent white box (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.55)`), rounded `18px`, strict `4:5` aspect ratio, with a floating 34px circular wishlist heart pinned to the top-right corner.
- **Typography & Details:** Product title clamped to 2 lines in Semibold `El Messiri`, followed by an amber rating star cluster. Price row displays primary price in Extrabold `IBM Plex Sans Arabic` (`#A02857`) alongside strike-through previous price in Truffle Taupe (`#8D7A80`).
- **Footer Action:** Anchored by the Quick-Add Card Button.

### Input Fields & Search
- **Search Capsule:** `50px` height, full pill shape (`999px`). Background is Soft Marshmallow Tint (`#FFF5F8`) with 1.5px Delicate Wafer border (`#F4E1E6`). Trailing edge integrates a circular strawberry trigger button. On focus, background turns pure white with a 5px ambient berry glow ring (`rgba(215, 45, 88, 0.08)`).
- **Form Inputs:** 48px height, rounded `14px`, Dark Cacao Ink typography, with labels rendered in IBM Plex Sans Arabic Bold.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Form Controls:** 22px custom circular controls for radio buttons and rounded `6px` squircle controls for checkboxes. Inactive state features a 1.5px Wafer Line with Soft Tint fill. Selected state fills with Deep Royal Berry (`#A02857`) and an inner white glyph.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Floating Mobile Dock Bar:** Fixed 12px above bottom screen margin with safe-area spacing. Surface is pure white with `24px` radius and Level 3 berry shadow. Houses navigation links and an elevated, oversized central Strawberry cart capsule badge.
- **Heart Flourish Divider:** Decorative center-aligned confectionery icon flanked by twin 50px hairline horizontal bars in Confectionery Rose (`#B85C6E`, 40% opacity).
- **Concierge Floating Action:** 52px WhatsApp concierge circle anchored at the lower-left edge (accounting for RTL primary flow at lower-right).
- **Top Announcement Ribbon:** Candy Blush background (`#FED7E7`) with centered berry text framed by decorative confectionery hearts.
